## Terminology
- XF - highest quality X-Mount lenses
- XC - budget oriented X-Mount lenses
- R - ring, electronically control the aperture diaphragm
- WR - weather resistance, moisture and dust seals
- OIS - optical image stabilization, very handy in those low light situations
- LM - linear motor, extremely quickly and almost silently
